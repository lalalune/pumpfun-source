## Method (all oracles, no build slot after the 11 raw-IR captures)

1. **Recapture.** w15a20's raw-IR oracle (`capraw.py`, `opt -O3`, slim, SDK `llc`), one capture per source variant
   (`tools/cap.sh`, `tools/capq.sh`, edit sets in `e/`, generated by `tools/mkedits.py` from w15a25 `g25.py`, w15a11
   `gen.py` and w15a20 `e/ov1a.py`). `m0` reproduces main (`eq_main: true`). `hc` and `e1` are w14a142's post-opt IR edits
   (`hphi`+`hcs`, plus `hev` for e1) on `m0/m.ll`.
2. **Placement: `tools/ares.py` + `tools/ar.sh`, an automatic join-restore.** It generalises w15a20 `restore.sh`/`mired2.py`
   and w14a142 `restore.sh`. On the `-stop-before=branch-folder` MIR it finds the post-sweep Result join `bb56.i*`, takes
   each live-in whose feeder defs are mostly one-slot reloads or `COPY`s (the Result payload, loaded from a different slot
   per feeder, is kept), deletes that def in all 12 feeders (6 direct predecessors plus the 6 that reach the join through
   `bb52.i*`), reloads the value at the head of `bb88`, and resumes llc at `branch-folder`. With no extra live-in (m0) the
   round trip is `cmp`-identical.
3. **Frame: `fobj.py` + `shperm.py` (w15a19).** `shperm.py <rx> 0x8e0 0x548 8` undoes the one-alloca band shift where
   there is one, then takes the best spill-colour permutation plus a swap climb. Without a shift this equals `permhc`'s Mhc.
4. **RA: a restore-aware version of w15a28 `pipe.sh` (`tools/rpipe.sh`, `tools/rpass2.sh`).**
   - `tools/colmap_r.py`: w14a84 `colmap.py`, but the votes come from the RESTORED build. The patched llc's vreg tags do
     not survive MIR serialisation, so they are copied from the unrestored tagged asm onto the restored asm by block
     content (3,100 of 3,283 instructions on T289n; all on m0).
   - `tools/llcw.py`: w15a24's sticky RAPRIO llc with `ares.py` between greedy and branch-folder, so every screened or
     climbed force set is scored at main's layout (`screen_r.py`, `sclimb_r.py` are w14a84's with this llc).
   - Joint: `permhc.py` (and `shperm.py` for shifted frames) on the final forced restored build.
