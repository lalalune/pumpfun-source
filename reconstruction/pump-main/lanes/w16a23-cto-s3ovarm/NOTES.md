## Why OV on S3 does not stack

1. **The accounts-pointer live-in is only half of the collapse.** S3n's event carrier removes it for the out-param
   forms (B1, B2, B3), the forwarded A1f/A0f and the o_rs forms A1r/A0r. The ov1a/ov1 families (A1, A0), B6 and C1
   keep a bb56.i live-in, and `ares.py` restores those. Even with main's placement, every cell's Mhc is 208-1,269 below
   S3n's 2,296. Its perfect-register-and-frame skeleton (`rf-`) is 11-135 tokens below S3n's -4.
2. **Where the best skeleton gains and loses.** B3 against S3n, true LCS rfj per orig chunk:
   - **Validate: +16** (0x800 +2, 0xc00 +6, 0x1400 +8).
     - B3 reproduces the original's piece copy of `new` at orig 0xc08-0xc38 (`add64 r7,1; ldxw [r7+3]; ldxw [r7+0]`),
       because validate reads `args.0`. This is the w13a41/w15a10 "Fa" entry-copy shape.
     - It also reproduces the target-piece copies and the holder-flag store `stxdw [r10-0x950]` at orig 0x1490-0x14e0.
   - **Sharing clone run: +6** (0x3c00). The out-param splits `su` into the original's 7 B and 9 B pieces
     (`sharing_update.sroa.3.sroa.0`/`.sroa.4`). B3 then reproduces the orig 0x3d28-0x3d80 piece copies
     (`[r10-0x550]`, `[r10-0x54d]`, `[r10-0x558]`).
   - **Post-validate tree: -16** (0x1800). The original keeps execute's tree at orig 0x1800-0x1aa0:
     - the shared test `ldxdw r1,[r10-0x920]; jne`;
     - the holder test `jeq r8,0`;
     - the class-F stores of the sharing tag `[r10-0x8f8]` (0x1838 from r0, 0x18d8 = 2, 0x1980 = 2);
     - the holder flag `[r10-0x950]` and the cashback flag `[r10-0x958]`;
     - the join at 0x1990.

     The OV forms delete this tree, so its tokens go unmatched.
   - **Event/tail: -12** (0x5800 -8, 0x5c00 -2, 0x6000 -2) and **sweep: -5** (0x3000). These are single reload and
     spill tokens (`data/mdiff-S3n-B3.txt`), i.e. RA noise.
3. **What the OV evidence really is.** In the original, the shared test inside the non-holder arm is at orig 0x1500:
   `ldxdw r1,[r10-0x928]; jeq r1,0,0x18f8`. It sits right after 307's success copies, and its not-shared edge enters
   the tree's non-shared arm at 0x18f8 (the `creator != default` memcmp of collect_old). So the original still has
   execute's tree, and the in-arm test is that tree's test threaded into validate's arm.
   - The threaded edge skips the tree's `holder_reward` test (0x18e0 `ldxdw r1,[r10-0x8e0]; jeq`), which the
     non-holder arm already knows is false (error 289). It re-stores tag 2 at 0x1978, the duplicated store.
   - This is the JumpThreading of the post-validate join that w15a20 and w15a27 priced (bb75: 10 memcpys of `t`,
     cost 8 > 6).
   - The B, C and D source forms move the decision out of the tree, so they lose it. The A forms keep the tree and
     add a second, unthreaded test in the arm. Neither reproduces the threaded edges.
4. **RA and frame.** The OV forms move registers further than S3n does (`r--` -191 to -483, against S3n's -168), and
   the climb does not recover it: B3's Mhc goes from 1,947 to 2,049 (+102), while S3n's goes from 2,296 to 2,347.
   - The out-param adds a 33 B alloca. B3's spill range is -0xc58..-0x8e8, against main's -0xc30..-0x8d8
     (`data/fobj-B3.txt`).
   - Keeping `let args=&(new,)` keeps w16a08's `%_45.sroa.5` 32 B copy (A1, A0, B3). Forwarding it (A1f, A0f, B1)
     gives a smaller frame shift but a worse skeleton.

## Handoff and next ideas

- **The B3 piece shapes are faithful structure, worth +22 rfj** (the entry copy of `new` in validate and the su 7/9
  copies in the clone run). They are worth pricing in a form that keeps execute's tree:
  - per-arm `su` writes inside the tree's arms rather than validate's, which keep the 7/9 split;
  - or validate reading `args.0` alone on S3n. On main this "Fa" form collapses (+184..+424 B, w16a03 mA,
    w16a21 c5F), but it has not been priced on S3n without OV.
- **OV on S3 done by the compiler** is unmeasured. Steps:
  1. capture S3n's raw IR;
  2. run JT2 with `-jump-threading-threshold=20`, or w15a27's `jtf.dylib` plugin with `JTF_BLOCKS=bb75`;
  3. restore main's layout with w15a27's `pjgen.py` and `mbp.sh`;
  4. run w16a08's `full.sh`.

  On main this bound was rfj -5 (w15a27 c2__pj). On S3n, the question is whether threading keeps the `1739=r9`
  payload force that gives S3n its edge.
- **Shim track (wave 17):** no `/*shim:*/` construct changes JumpThreading's BBDupThreshold. The only spelling that gets
  bb75 under the threshold is a by-reference or raw-pointer return of `target` (the w15a27 s27 shape, 0.299 @ 27,024),
  and it loses more than 100 rfj. A volatile or black_box store of the tag inside validate's arm would add frame
  objects that the original does not have: the original's `[r10-0x8f8]` is a spilled phi of the tree, not a memory
  variable. Neither is a lever for this angle.
